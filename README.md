# Awesome-Sales-Pipeline-Inspection

## Top Sales Pipeline Inspection Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Pipeline Visibility, Deal Inspection, Forecast Accuracy, Activity Capture, Hygiene Monitoring & Revenue Intelligence*

**Last updated: August 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Sales Pipeline Inspection**. These tools help revenue teams inspect deal health, surface risks, improve CRM hygiene, capture activity automatically, analyze pipeline bottlenecks, and produce more accurate forecasts.



**Examples** include Scratchpad, People.ai (Backstory), Clari, Revenue Grid, BoostUp (Terret), Salesken, Attention, Revenue.io, Dooly, and Pipeline CRM (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosted CRMs with strong pipeline views, deal tracking, activity logging, and emerging open revenue-intelligence or signal tools — ideal for RevOps, sales leaders, and teams seeking transparency and control over pipeline data.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Scratchpad](https://www.scratchpad.com/)**  

  Salesforce-native workspace that provides a fast, spreadsheet-style pipeline grid, real-time sync, and AI hygiene monitoring to surface stalled or incomplete deals.



- **[People.ai / Backstory](https://www.people.ai/)**  

  Activity capture and revenue intelligence platform that automatically maps emails, meetings, and calls to accounts and opportunities for cleaner pipeline visibility.



- **[Clari](https://www.clari.com/)**  

  Leading revenue intelligence and pipeline inspection platform offering deal inspection, forecasting, activity capture, and executive-level pipeline visibility (now expanded with Salesloft capabilities).



- **[Revenue Grid](https://www.revenuegrid.com/)**  

  Salesforce-centric sales engagement and revenue intelligence solution with guided selling, activity tracking, and pipeline insights.



- **[BoostUp / Terret](https://www.boostup.ai/)**  

  AI revenue intelligence platform focused on deal-level risk scoring, pipeline analytics, and forecasting for mid-market and enterprise teams.



- **[Salesken](https://www.salesken.ai/)**  

  Conversation and sales intelligence platform that provides real-time guidance and insights useful for pipeline and deal inspection.



- **[Attention](https://www.attention.tech/)**  

  AI-powered sales platform offering conversation intelligence and pipeline-related insights for modern revenue teams.



- **[Revenue.io](https://www.revenue.io/)**  

  Real-time sales engagement and conversation AI platform that supports live guidance and revenue workflow visibility.



- **[Dooly](https://www.dooly.ai/)**  

  Collaborative deal workspace that helps teams update CRM fields, capture notes, and inspect opportunities more efficiently (especially in Salesforce environments).



- **[Pipeline CRM](https://www.pipelinecrm.com/)**  

  Sales-focused CRM with visual pipeline management, reporting, and tools aimed at improving deal visibility and process discipline.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Meow](https://github.com/nash-md/meow)**  

  Open-source visual sales pipeline dashboard and CRM with configurable stages, Kanban views, and forecasting-style insights into current pipeline value.



- **[Pipelite](https://github.com/Bittencourt/pipelite)**  

  Self-hostable CRM featuring kanban pipeline management, activity tracking, custom fields, organizations, people, and deal boards.



- **[Frappe CRM](https://github.com/frappe/crm)**  

  Open-source, self-hostable CRM with unlimited users, sales pipelines, REST API, and modern pipeline management capabilities.



- **[Twenty](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty)**  

  Modern open-source CRM built for the community, offering clean pipeline and deal management features.



- **[SuiteCRM](https://github.com/salesagility/SuiteCRM)**  

  Popular full-featured open-source CRM with sales automation, pipeline tracking, and extensive customization options.



- **[EspoCRM](https://github.com/espocrm/espocrm)**  

  Open-source CRM with a modern interface, powerful API, and solid support for sales pipelines and activities.



- **[Beton Inspector](https://github.com/getbeton/inspector)**  

  Open-source revenue intelligence tool that detects buying signals from product analytics (e.g., PostHog) and CRM data to score and route warm accounts.



- **[OpenCloser / AI sales platforms](https://github.com/)**  

  Desktop or self-hosted AI sales platforms that include Kanban CRM, call analysis, coaching, and pipeline-oriented workflows.



- **[Radiant AI CRM / similar agentic CRMs](https://github.com/)**  

  Emerging open-source AI-first CRMs that analyze activity, suggest next actions, and help keep deals moving.



- **[Lightweight pipeline CRMs (Prospex, open-crm, Investor-CRM, etc.)](https://github.com/)**  

  Community projects focused on visual pipelines, contact management, and simple deal tracking suitable for small teams or specialized use cases.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Nextcloud / community CRM modules with drag-and-drop pipelines and sales dashboards.

- Custom dbt or SQL models that inspect CRM data for hygiene issues, stage aging, and coverage gaps.

- n8n / workflow automation recipes that flag stalled deals and missing next steps.

- Activity logging and email/calendar sync projects that improve CRM data quality.

- Open forecasting notebooks and simple weighted-pipeline calculators built on CRM exports.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Start with a self-hosted CRM that has strong pipeline views (**Frappe CRM**, **Twenty**, **Pipelite**, or **Meow**), layer activity capture via email/calendar integrations or open sync tools, add signal detection (**Beton Inspector** or custom product-analytics rules), and surface risks with SQL/dbt models or simple dashboards. Local LLMs can help summarize deal health, suggest next actions, and generate inspection notes for managers.
